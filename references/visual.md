# QuantCorner report design

Use the authentic bundled mark unchanged, preserving its separate green evidence point and clear space at least its diameter. No generated/reconstructed logos. The mark identifies this reporting template, not Webull or QuantCorner endorsement of a strategy.

Light canvas #FAFAF7, white surfaces, text #212121 and #616161. Primary #6200EE for the strategy series; secondary ink #00796B for its benchmark. Roboto and Noto Sans Thai bundled WOFF2 fonts; no CDN requests. Keep the report typographic and chart-led, not a wall of colorful cards. No animation is needed for static research evidence.

Equity and drawdown must be computed from actual result rows, with dates, units, legend and definition. Show source/as-of, requested versus returned period, costs, price basis, corporate-action policy, benchmark and sample count. Never smooth away bad periods or truncate drawdown to make it appear shallow. Preserve all observations in the SVG; distinguish empty/undefined metrics from zero.

The report includes return, CAGR, volatility, MDD, Sharpe, Sortino, win rate, payoff, expectancy and profit factor explanations. Undefined denominators show N/A. Add recovery duration or other diagnostics only when actually computed and checked. Close-to-close observations do not capture all intraday risk.

Inspect desktop and narrow mobile, escaping untrusted content and testing long titles/constant or tiny samples. Reports must work offline and print to PDF. Synthetic examples carry an unmistakable banner. Do not publish real-data reports without specific sharing authorization and rights.
